03 / About me

Hello, I’m Iasmina,

interior designer and founder of ISM Unique Interior Design Studio.

I studied interior design at UNAGE in Iași and in Milan, experiences that shaped my design perspective and refined my eye for aesthetics and detail.

For me, every project is an opportunity to create a space that combines elegance and functionality, tailored to each client’s personality and lifestyle.
